# Code of conduct

We want this project to support rigorous, constructive research collaboration.
Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence and implementation rather than attacking people;
- state uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest honestly;
- respect confidentiality, authorship, licenses, and research-participant privacy;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct;
- give credit for ideas, code, data, and review contributions;
- correct material errors promptly and preserve an auditable explanation.

Unacceptable conduct may be removed from project spaces, and repeat or severe
violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository owner's contact channel. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the
people involved.

Maintainers must apply these standards consistently, disclose relevant
conflicts, and recuse themselves when they cannot review a report impartially.
