CAPABILITIES

Coordinate the whole paper without blurring the evidence.

The workflow controls dependencies and handoffs. Specialist skills remain responsible for literature, proofs, empirical results, simulations, and manuscript claims.

DIRECTION + IDEAS

Turn a broad topic into competing mechanisms

  • Classify the paper and freeze the immediate objective.
  • Generate materially different candidates rather than title variants.
  • Attach nearest-neighbor searches, cheap falsifiers, and reopen conditions.
LITERATURE + CLAIMS

Keep novelty language inside the retrieved evidence

  • Separate discovery, screening, deep reading, and citation verification.
  • Expose journal, citation-graph, terminology, and mechanism coverage gaps.
  • Keep novelty pending when influential neighbors remain unresolved.
DESIGN + THEORY

Bind assumptions and theorems to intended claims

  • Freeze estimands, identification logic, assumptions, and comparison sets.
  • Route theorem work to installed specialists through explicit interfaces.
  • Preserve hash-bound proof lineage without duplicating proof truth.
EMPIRICAL + SIMULATION

Separate setup, pilot evidence, and production results

  • Record data provenance, diagnostics, robustness, and result permissions.
  • Distinguish smoke, pilot, and production numerical work.
  • Persist offline-job handles and continue only result-independent work.
WRITING + REVIEW

Prevent prose from outrunning the paper

  • Continue source-bounded structure while blocking unsupported sentences.
  • Review scientific dependencies separately from schema validity.
  • Use non-regression contracts, paired evidence, and rollback conditions.
STATE + RELEASE

Resume cleanly and release only what was verified

  • Persist stage status, blocker, next safe action, and resume condition.
  • Keep specialist artifacts authoritative through hash-bound handoffs.
  • Package deterministic artifacts with checksums, provenance, and limitations.

TRUST THE BOUNDARIES

Designed to expose uncertainty, not decorate it.

The workflow organizes evidence and handoffs. It does not guarantee novelty, mathematical correctness, identification, publication, or exhaustive coverage.

Resumable state

Versioned records preserve the next action, blocker, and resume condition across sessions.

Proof lineage interface

A public hash-bound framework connects theory stages without bundling private theorem content.

Non-regression contracts

Material revisions state what must improve, what cannot regress, and when to roll back.

Reproducible package

Deterministic archives include internal hashes, external checksums, CI, and provenance attestations.
