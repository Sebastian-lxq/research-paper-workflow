# Usage profiles

These prompts are examples, not additional authorization or evidence.

## Resume an existing paper

```text
Use $research-paper-workflow to inspect the current manuscript, project
instructions, scientific ledgers, and workflow receipts. Resume at the earliest
stale or unfinished requirement, avoid rebuilding completed work, and continue
unaffected branches while reporting any missing capability.
```

## Develop candidate ideas

```text
Use $research-paper-workflow to turn this broad direction into materially
different candidate mechanisms. For each retained candidate, record the closest
verified neighbors, a de-anchored search path, the cheapest discriminating
falsifier, feasibility evidence, a stop or continuation reason, and an
evidence-based reopen condition. Do not certify novelty.
```

## Simulation-deferred paper

```text
Use $research-paper-workflow to complete every part of the paper that does not
depend on production simulation results. Deliver the design, runnable simulation
handoff, resource estimate, resume procedure, expected result schema, and pending
claim map. Do not represent pilot or missing results as confirmatory evidence.
```

## Continuous bounded revision

```text
Use $research-paper-workflow to freeze the paper objective and non-regression
contract, then process one admitted issue at a time until the selected cycle
converges or a real author decision is required. Reassess the whole issue queue
after each retained change and finish with a whole-candidate review.
```

## Optional governed expert review

```text
Use $research-paper-workflow with the explicitly selected expert-supervision
provider. Freeze its scope, exact artifacts, evidence-access boundary, and
decision target. Preserve the provider's authorization and state rules; do not
open private sources or start a roundtable merely because the workflow is active.
```

The public package does not contain private expert profiles. Users who connect a
private provider must follow that provider's own consent, access, and egress
contract.
